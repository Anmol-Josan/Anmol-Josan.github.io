## Reconciliations with the brief

- **425 Tutoring:** $120,000 is “received in funding” in the PDF, so the site uses “funding received.” 3,200+ students, 19 countries, 45 states, 2 territories, 14 management-team members, and 95% retention are retained.
- **EPS Hack Club:** $10,000 savings and $2,000 funding are separate; the site does not claim $12k+ raised. The PDF also reports 300% membership growth, 400% female participation growth, 25 members, 6 projects, 600+ daily users, 90 hackathon participants, and 36 tons of CO₂ avoided yearly.
- **Cancer research:** 93% accuracy concerns prediction of treatment response for hormone receptor-positive breast cancer, not diagnosis. The PDF says “pre-published” in JMIR. No final-publication status, paper title, DOI, or clinical validation was invented.
- **USACO:** the activity description says top 200 nationwide; eligibility text says internationally. The site follows the activity description and records that discrepancy in the source disclosure.
- **WA Science Fair:** 1st place is explicitly computational biology. The PDF's 1,200-student reference is retained only in contextual notes because it does not clearly establish the category-specific denominator.
- **Berkeley MET:** $7.5M is a valuation by judges, not money raised; qualification appears directly in the metric label.
- **Kiddie Academies:** 200 hours/week saved remains explicitly estimated.
- **YC Startup School:** the <5% figure is attributed to the program described by the source, not the YC accelerator.
- **Overlapping metrics:** Hack Club and EPCarpool share the 36-ton figure. Project user populations can overlap. There is no combined reach, funding, or savings total.
- **Climbing:** V9 and 5.13d are retained; top 1% is marked as a source claim, with no independent ranking methodology supplied.
- **RC aircraft:** 4.5 feet describes model size. The source does not distinguish wingspan from length, so neither is asserted.
- **Unquantified entries:** EduLens, EPS Ultimate, and the debate bid use their actual named outcome instead of invented adoption or ranking numbers.

The source is an application draft. Its historical metrics are not presented as current live measurements.
